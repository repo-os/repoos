## Problem

A fresh repo shows a bare dashboard: no guidance, no next step. First-run
users don't know that `repoos init` scaffolds AGENTS.md, or that they can drag
a task straight into the queue.

## Desired UX

- Each empty panel shows a one-line hint with a keyboard shortcut.
- The work queue gets a friendly "create your first task" state with a CTA.
