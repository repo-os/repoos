
While you work, keep a running file called `FRICTION-LOG.md` in the current directory. Add an entry every time something about RepoOS (install, CLI, docs, error messages, the task workflow, the web UI, naming, defaults) was confusing, surprising, slow, broken, or made you stop and think. Be specific and honest; include exact commands and error text. Also note things that worked well.

When you finish, add a `## Debrief` section to the same file: did you reach a working app, roughly how many steps went to fighting RepoOS rather than building, and what the top three improvements to RepoOS would be.
