## Problem
When a task successfully moves to done, there's no strong visual signal — it's easy for the human to miss that a task finished successfully, especially with several tasks in flight.

## Fix
Flash/highlight a task card when it lands in done, and keep that highlight until the human acknowledges it — e.g. a small "acknowledge"/dismiss button at the bottom of the card, similar in style to the action buttons already used on cards in other statuses.
